# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play. What is true that makes this decision necessary. Constraints, not opinions.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated in the active voice.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the section future-you actually reads.

## Consequences

What this makes easy. What this makes hard. What we are now committed to. Include the bad parts;
an ADR with no downsides listed is marketing.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** ... **when** ... **then** ...

(These compile into tests. If a criterion cannot become a test, it is not yet a criterion.)
